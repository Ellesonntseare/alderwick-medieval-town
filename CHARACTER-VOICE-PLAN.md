# Character intelligence and realistic voices

User requirements recorded 3 November 2026:

- Realistic spoken voices for conversations between the player and villagers.
- Smart villagers with their own decisions, activities, personalities, and conversations with other villagers.
- Audible conversations between NPCs that the player can overhear while passing nearby.
- Voices originate at each speaking character's world position. They become quieter with distance and fade out beyond hearing range, smoothly as the player moves.

Implemented in the local build:

- Navigation, collision, jobs, fixed guard duty, door queues, and bed schedules run locally. Farmers deliver visible vegetables, carpenters create furniture, and couriers carry supplies. Every shield-bearing resident has a fixed daytime guard post and sleeps at night.
- Personal conversations are generated by a local Qwen2.5-3B-Instruct model using the resident's background, relationships, real activity and saved chat history. The side panel accepts freely typed messages. Nearby NPC exchanges request generated alternating turns. Only reciprocal, active partners can speak; ending or interrupting a conversation cancels stale audio requests and playback.
- Eight Kokoro neural English voice profiles are assigned consistently across residents. The runtime is a separate local copy under `tools/speech`; the existing Elleson Studio installation is unchanged. It starts automatically, synthesizes on the CPU, and caches generated turns. No paid provider or external speech requests are required.
- Mono speech plays through an AudioStreamPlayer3D attached near the speaking resident's head. Inverse-distance attenuation and a 26 m maximum range make nearby exchanges audible and distant ones fade away. A dedicated AudioListener3D follows the player's head in first and third person. Overhead street listening stays at the player; direct overhead dialogue focuses the listener near the selected resident so distant camera zoom does not mute a reply.
- Paired speech prefetches the next turn while the current one plays. Direct dialogue splits longer answers into spoken chunks. A first uncached line can take a few seconds to synthesize on this PC.
- The Character voices checkbox controls character speech. M controls the background music. Music plays at -34 dB and ducks to -40 dB during direct dialogue. Conversation text remains visible in the dialogue panel or overhead bubbles.

- `work-preview-output.log` records actual neural speech playback in the native game.
- `VOICE-CREDITS.md` lists the runtime, model, voice provenance, and retained notices.

Remaining scope:

- Dialogue is generated locally. Navigation, schedules, jobs and transactions remain local simulation rules; generative AI control of physical actions is not connected.
- Live microphone input and speech recognition are not connected. Player dialogue uses freely typed messages in the scrolling side panel, without preset topic buttons.
- Future AI integration must retain valid navigation actions, guard schedules, turn-taking, personal memory, and positional audio rather than bypassing them.
