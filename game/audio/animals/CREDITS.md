# Animal sound sources

Cow and pig: recordings by Secretlondon, Mudchute Farm animals, CC BY-SA 3.0 or later. https://opengameart.org/content/farm-animals ; https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/ . Runtime files converted to mono WAV, trimmed to at most 3.5 seconds and loudness-normalized. These derived audio files remain under CC BY-SA 3.0. Original archive and info.txt are retained here.

Horse hoof sounds: Alan McKinney (alanmcki), distributed by AntumDeluge, CC BY 3.0. https://opengameart.org/content/horse-gallop-loop ; https://freesound.org/s/403026/ ; https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/ . Runtime clip shortened to 1.4 seconds, converted to mono WAV, and loudness-normalized.

Dog bark: Brandon Morris, CC0. https://opengameart.org/content/dog-barking-mono . Converted to mono WAV, limited to 3.5 seconds, loudness-normalized.

Sheep baa: AntumDeluge, CC0 as declared on https://opengameart.org/content/sheep-baa . Converted to mono WAV, limited to 3.5 seconds, loudness-normalized.
